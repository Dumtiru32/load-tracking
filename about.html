Load Tracking is a web-based application designed to enhance visibility and coordination between truck drivers and dispatchers during the freight transportation process.

Truck driver is a trucking company or owner-operator that transports freight for shippers commercially

The platform enables real-time tracking of active shipments through GPS integration or manual status updates submitted by drivers. Each load's status, location, and estimated time of arrival (ETA) are available to dispatchers through a centralized dashboard, allowing for efficient load management and proactive decision-making.
Truck drivers can easily update key milestones such as departure, delays, checkpoints, and delivery completion via a mobile-friendly interface. This minimizes the need for manual check-ins and reduces delays caused by communication gaps.

Dispatcher A person responsible for coordinating truck drivers, assigning loads, planning routes, and ensuring timely deliveries. Dispatchers act as the communication hub between drivers, shippers, and consignees.


For dispatchers, Load Tracking offers tools to monitor fleet activity, optimize routing, and improve customer service through accurate, real-time insights into load progress.

By bridging the gap between drivers and dispatchers, Load Tracking supports supply chain transparency, improves operational efficiency, and facilitates data-driven logistics planning.
